# Compliance

This document tracks how Sentio Dental OS meets each compliance requirement in Build Prompt §7. It is updated in every phase.

Where the law needs interpreting, the product uses **configurable settings** set to the safest reasonable default. These defaults are listed under "Decisions taken without a legal review".

**Status legend:**

- ✅ Built and tested
- 🟡 Partly built
- ⏳ Planned (phase shown)

## Roles

- **Clinic:** Data Fiduciary.
- **Sentio Care Pvt Ltd:** Data Processor.

The data processing agreement (DPA) signed at purchase has to reflect this split. In the product, the split shows up like this:

- the clinic's owner approves consent notices, templates and campaigns;
- the clinic handles patient data requests from its dashboard;
- Sentio staff can only see operational data (health, billing, usage) in the admin panel, not patient records, unless the clinic grants support access for a limited time.

## Decisions taken without a legal review

On 27 Sep 2026 the founder decided not to get a legal review and approved all compliance decisions. Each one below uses the **safest reasonable default** and stays configurable, so it can be changed later without code changes.

| #   | Question                                                           | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **(D1)** Where compute runs                                        | All data at rest stays in Supabase Mumbai: database, files, recordings, backups. The API and worker run in Railway's closest region to India and store nothing. The DPDP Act restricts transfers only to countries the government blacklists, and we keep none at rest abroad anyway.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2   | **(D5)** LLM outside India                                         | Allowed only after PII is removed (names, phone numbers, dates of birth and addresses replaced with placeholders). The provider must not train on our data. Sub-processors are listed in the clinic agreement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 3   | **(D6)** TRAI / DLT                                                | Transactional SMS fallback is sent only through a DLT-registered provider with registered templates and a registered sender ID (the adapter refuses anything else). AI outbound calls are **transactional only** (reminders, confirmations, treatment continuity for existing patients) and only from the telephony provider's registered numbers, 09:00–20:00. **Promotional AI calls are disabled for every clinic.** Only a Sentio admin can turn them on, after registration is done. Promotional WhatsApp needs explicit opt-in.                      |
| 4   | **(A-9)** Auto-recharge rules                                      | Pre-debit notice at least 24 hours before every debit. Automatic debits capped at ₹15,000; anything larger goes by payment link.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 5   | **Retention** (defaults, per clinic, auto-purged by a nightly job) | Call recordings: 90 days. Transcripts and WhatsApp messages: 1 year. Clinical records and appointments: kept while the clinic uses the product, and at least 3 years after the last visit. Invoices, receipts and the usage ledger: 8 years (tax records). Deleted-patient requests remove personal details but keep anonymised billing rows the law requires.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6   | **Consent notice**                                                 | Texts below. Shown or spoken at first contact. Every consent is stored with the notice version.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 7   | **GST codes**                                                      | License and usage are invoiced as separate lines, each with a configurable SAC code. Sentio's accountant confirms the codes and rate before the first invoice. Patient receipts default to GST-exempt (healthcare), per procedure setting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 8   | **WhatsApp consent**                                               | The first reply to a new number is the notice below, with an "I agree" button. Tapping it, **or writing again after reading it**, is recorded as consent (a clear affirmative act under DPDP §6), with the notice version, language and the message that gave it. "No" stops the assistant and asks staff to call. Consent records can never be edited or deleted. Marketing needs its own explicit yes. STOP blocks all reminders and campaigns at once, but the clinic can still answer if the patient writes.                                           |
| 9   | **What the assistant may say**                                     | Only fixed, reviewed texts. It never gives a diagnosis, medicine names or doses, and never quotes a price the clinic has not entered. A final safety filter checks every outgoing message and replaces anything unsafe with a hand-off to staff. Emergencies (swelling spreading to the eye or neck, breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, trauma) always reach a person: a critical task, an alert, and the clinic's phone number to the patient.                                                                                                            |
| 10  | **Phone calls: recording and consent**                             | Every call answered by the assistant starts with the notice below, which cannot be talked over. Continuing to speak after it is recorded as consent to recording (`call_recording`, notice `voice-v1`, stored with the call). Recordings are copied from the phone company into our storage in India and deleted after 90 days by a nightly job. Callers can ask for a person at any time ("staff se baat", or press 0).                                                                                                                                   |
| 11  | **Phone calls: what the assistant may say**                        | The same rules as WhatsApp (decision 9): only fixed, reviewed sentences, every one checked by the safety filter before it is spoken. Medicine and diagnosis questions are politely refused with an offer of a consultation. Prices only from the clinic's approved list. Emergencies: a fixed script (with 112 advice when breathing or swallowing is affected), then the call is put through to the doctors in emergency order, then staff, then the clinic phone, with WhatsApp alerts and a critical task. The eval suite enforces all of this at 100%. |

### Consent notice (version 1)

**WhatsApp, English:**

> Namaste! You are chatting with {clinic}'s digital assistant. {clinic} uses your name, phone number and appointment details only to manage your dental care: bookings, reminders, receipts and follow-ups. Messages may be processed by our technology partner Sentio Care on the clinic's behalf. Reply STOP anytime to stop messages. Details: {privacy_link}

**WhatsApp, Hindi:**

> नमस्ते! आप {clinic} की डिजिटल असिस्टेंट से बात कर रहे हैं। {clinic} आपका नाम, फ़ोन नंबर और अपॉइंटमेंट की जानकारी सिर्फ़ आपके दाँतों के इलाज के लिए इस्तेमाल करता है: बुकिंग, रिमाइंडर, रसीद और फ़ॉलो-अप। यह जानकारी क्लिनिक की ओर से हमारे टेक्नोलॉजी पार्टनर Sentio Care द्वारा प्रोसेस की जा सकती है। मैसेज बंद करने के लिए कभी भी STOP लिखें। विवरण: {privacy_link}

**Voice (spoken at the start of every call, notice version `voice-v1`; it cannot be interrupted):**

> नमस्ते, {clinic} में फ़ोन करने के लिए धन्यवाद। मैं क्लिनिक की डिजिटल असिस्टेंट हूँ। यह कॉल क्वालिटी के लिए रिकॉर्ड हो सकती है, और आपकी जानकारी सिर्फ़ आपके इलाज के लिए इस्तेमाल होगी।

> Namaste, thank you for calling {clinic}. I'm the clinic's digital assistant. This call may be recorded for quality, and your details are used only for your treatment.

The **privacy link** points to a per-clinic privacy page, generated from the clinic's details. It lists:

- the purposes,
- the retention periods above,
- how to withdraw consent or ask for data deletion (reply on WhatsApp, or ask at the front desk), and
- the clinic's grievance contact.
